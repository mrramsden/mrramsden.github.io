The prince has been captured by an evil madman and only our daring heroine can save him. He is locked in one of 15 rooms and is rapidly running out of oxygen: the princess has time to check just 6 rooms before it is too late. Armed with a shield (3 charges) and a laser pistol (100 power), she sets out to rescue him.

The rooms

  • 15 rooms in total
  • 7 are empty
  • 7 hold a killer robot
  • 1 holds the prince

Combat

  • Robots have a shield of 5 to 30
  • The princess shoots first, using as much energy as the gun has left
  • If her shot uses more energy than the robot's shield, it is destroyed
  • If not, the robot fires back and she loses a shield charge

Win or lose

  • Win: she finds the prince in time
  • Lose: she opens 6 rooms without finding him
  • Lose: she is shot 4 times (3 shields plus 1 life)
