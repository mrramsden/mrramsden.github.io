Robot Brain Challenges

Next lesson, a micro:bit becomes the brain of a robot. Get your MakeCode skills back by building four of the jobs that brain will need to do.

Trophies 🥉 🥈 🥇 💎 0 of 4 done

Before you start

  1. Go to makecode.microbit.org and click New Project. Put your name in the project title.
  2. Test everything in the simulator on the left of the screen. You only need a real micro:bit if your teacher hands one out.
  3. Work through the challenges in order. When your program does something on the list, tick it off. Tick everything and you win the trophy.

All four trophies!

Your micro:bit can signal, steer, start a race and take orders by radio. That's a robot brain. Next challenge: add a third message to your remote control that shows a cross when you press A and B together.

Your ticks are saved in this browser on this computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.
